Unity Music Studio

VOLT

Make a beat, one step at a time. Four beats × four subdivisions = sixteen steps.

Start with your device volume low. No microphone, audio uploads or accounts. Switching tabs or leaving stops sound; press Start to resume. Timing varies by device.

Stopped. Press Start when ready.

Press a numbered pad to toggle a hit. Keyboard: Tab to a control; Enter or Space toggles it. Arrow keys move between pads. Scroll each row sideways on a small screen. Gold lines mark beat boundaries.

Choosing a style alone changes nothing. Load style replaces hits, tempo and swing, unmutes all tracks, and keeps your level. These editable synthetic rhythm sketches are inspired starting points, not authentic genre representations.

One pattern is saved only in this browser. It can be seen or changed by the next person using this device. Erase saved VOLT data before leaving. This does not erase other Unity site data. There is no recording or export.
