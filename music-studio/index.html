VOLT — Make a beat

Four synthesized drum sounds. Sixteen steps. Create a repeating rhythm with touch, mouse or keyboard.

Sound starts only when you press Start. No microphone, account, upload or payment. Your pattern stays in this browser; reset it on a shared device.

Try together

  1. Start at a low speaker or headphone volume.
  2. Count four beats; each beat has four steps.
  3. Move one drum hit. Describe how the rhythm changes.

This is an exploratory exercise, not a graded music assessment.
